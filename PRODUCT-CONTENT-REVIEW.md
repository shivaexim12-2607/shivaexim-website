# Product-content improvements — 12 October 2026

The owner supplied 21 competitor websites and asked to preserve Shiva Exim's current design while improving product details. Accessible home pages were reviewed for catalogue structure; detailed cumin, fennel and sesame pages were inspected for parameter ideas. Ramya Global and Neatpick could not be retrieved by the research tool. Accura's supplied apex URL failed, but its www Spices page was retrieved through search. Other accessible sites vary in useful detail and some primarily concern psyllium, which is not in the authorized current catalogue.

The strongest transferable ideas were specification tables, distinctions between product forms, practical applications and more useful enquiry information. Competitor numeric limits, facilities, processing claims, certification badges and medicinal claims were not adopted as Shiva Exim facts. No product was added solely because a competitor lists it.

## Implemented

- Original product-specific questions on all 29 product pages; deeper coverage for cumin, fennel, alfalfa, onion seed and sesame.
- Added crop-year/origin requirements and tailored technical parameters to each product's existing specification guide.
- Cumin/fennel: ash, microbiological and residue requirements; confirmed 99% commercial options retained. Botanical/local names reference Spices Board catalogue entries.
- Alfalfa: hard seed, other-seed screening, lot/test identity and coating declarations. Single-year/multi-year commercial labels remain separate from named-variety, stand-life and certification claims. Generic seed-test concepts are supported by ISTA and the University of Minnesota seed-label guidance; no foreign seed-law limits are applied to India.
- Onion seed: crop/variety suitability, lot/moisture requirements and distinction from culinary kalonji.
- Sesame: natural/hulled distinction, residual hull and defects, oil/FFA and required lot-test scope. These are enquiry parameters, not confirmed processing capabilities or numeric stock limits.
- All products: packing, handling/shelf-life details to confirm and a clear buyer/supplier enquiry checklist.
- Fixed the generic specification-status copy so products without company-confirmed commercial quality options do not imply such options have been verified.
